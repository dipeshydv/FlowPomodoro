Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Your Privacy is Our Priority

FlowPomodoro is built with privacy as a foundational principle. We do not run third-party advertising trackers, sell user data, or require account creation to use our Pomodoro focus tools.

What Data We Collect

We collect no personally identifiable data. FlowPomodoro stores your focus timer history, task items, personal settings, and streaks exclusively in your browser's local storage (localStorage). This data stays entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate, anonymized metrics (such as page views and general traffic trends) to help improve performance. This data contains no personally identifiable information. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.

Cookies & Local Storage

FlowPomodoro does not use marketing or advertising tracking cookies. We utilize standard browser localStorage purely to remember your timer preferences, task items, and dark/light theme choice.

Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data. To permanently delete all stored data, simply clear your browser's local storage for flowpomodoro.xyz in your browser settings or developer tools.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our team at: privacy@flowpomodoro.xyz
