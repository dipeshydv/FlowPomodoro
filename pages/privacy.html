Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Your Privacy, Guaranteed

FlowPomodoro is built with privacy as a core principle. We don't run ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't require you to create an account.

What Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. FlowPomodoro stores all your session history, tasks, settings, and streaks exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate site usage (page views, session counts). This data is anonymized and contains no personally identifiable information. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

FlowPomodoro does not use cookies for tracking. Google Analytics may set standard analytics cookies. No advertising cookies are used.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts and Font Awesome for typography and icons. These services may log standard browser request metadata (IP address, user agent) per their own privacy policies.

Data Deletion

To delete all your FlowPomodoro data, clear your browser's localStorage for this site. In Chrome: DevTools → Application → Local Storage → Clear All.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email: privacy@flowpomodoro.xyz
