Flow state is described by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi as a state of complete immersion in an activity. Achieving this state requires a balance between challenge and skill.

The Science of Flow

Using a pomodoro timer effectively breaks down barriers to entry, allowing you to enter flow more consistently.

Key Takeaway

You don't need to force yourself into 25-minute sessions. Start with an interval that feels easy to begin, then gradually increase it.

Setting Up Your Environment

Your physical environment dictates your mental state. Remove all visual distractions. Turn off phone notifications using Do Not Disturb mode.

Small, consistent sessions are often easier to sustain than ambitious bursts of productivity.

Advanced Techniques

Combine 25-minute sprints with binaural beats for maximum effect. Our app supports this workflow natively.
